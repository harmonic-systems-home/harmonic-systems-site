      Scope of web page

      This web page is dedicated to Eric and Ellen Wilson's familes. This first version is mostly limited to their direct ancestry.

      This page presents about 400 photos of about 65 family members.

      I have attempted to provide photos of each person at two-year intervals for their first 20 years, and ten-year intervals thereafter. I have also included photos of homes, cars, work and hobbies, when possible.

      Maintenance

      There are many errors and omissions, to be sure. Please let me know if you have any photos to add, or errors to correct.

      Future Versions

      I hope to continue adding to this page indefinately. I would like to add the aunts, uncles and cousins. I would like to add audio and video material. I would like to fill in gaps in what I already have. This is mostly limited by my spare time, which is scarce!

      What You Can Do

      If you have material for this web page, there are several options.

      For photos, I would perfer to get them already scanned (max 7.5" high, 10" wide, 16 bit color, PICT or JPG format). If that is not possible, I can scan them.

      For videos, we'll have to wait until I know more about transfering video into web pages!

      For documents, I would prefer to have these in a word-processor format, such as Microsoft Word, or an exchange format, such as RTF. I would really like to get more diaries, or biographies of the family. Anything you would like to compose I'd enjoy including.

      I welcome any and all other suggestions. Please let me know what you think.
