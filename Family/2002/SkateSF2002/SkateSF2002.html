Skate San Francisco 2002


Ellen's last ice skating competition of the year was at the Yerba Buena Ice Rink in downtown San Francisco. This is a very pretty rink in a not-so-nice part of town

Ellen skating a good program, falling on just one double-toe-loop. She placed 6th out of 8, but was happy with her performance. She was skating against a strong set of competitors, who came in from LA, Los Vegas, Seattle and other distant lands.
