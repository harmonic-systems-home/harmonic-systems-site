Stamps on the Postcards

Postcards to Fred Wright, 1904–1918

The stamps on the cards, oldest issue first. Each kind is shown once at full scan resolution (click to open it alone), then as it appears on every card that carries it, cancels and all; each small stamp links to its card. Catalogue numbers are the Scott numbers for the design; printings, perforations and watermarks are not told apart.
